PicText サポート

写真に短い文字を追加し、位置・大きさ・回転を調整して保存できます。

文字を編集する

写真を選び、「文字を追加」で入力します。配置済みの文字をタップすると選択でき、もう一度タップすると再編集できます。

画像を保存できない場合

iOSの「設定」でPicTextの写真への追加を許可してください。空き容量も確認してください。

プライバシーポリシー

PicText Support

Add short captions to photos, adjust their position, size and rotation, and save the result.

Edit text

Choose a photo and tap Add Text. Tap a text item to select it, then tap it again to edit. Use the color controls to choose white, black, yellow or red. Use the size slider or gestures to adjust the selected text.

Save a photo

Tap the save icon. If saving is not allowed, enable photo-library additions for PicText in iOS Settings. Also check your available storage.

Language

Version 1.1 supports English and Japanese. The app uses your device's preferred supported language. You can choose an app language in iOS Settings when that option is available.

Advertising

Editing works even when no ad is available or tracking permission is declined. Ad Privacy Settings appears when the consent service provides that option.

Contact

Please include a description of the problem, your device model, app version and iOS version. You do not need to send photos or other personal information.
