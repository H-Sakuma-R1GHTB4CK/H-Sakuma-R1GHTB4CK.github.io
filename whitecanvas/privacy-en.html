White Canvas Privacy Policy

Photos and image editing

You choose an image using the system photo picker, edit it on your device, and save it to your photo library. White Canvas does not send your photos to the developer or to advertising service servers. Saving requires permission to add photos. You can still edit images if you decline saving permission.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP). Device identifiers such as advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from your IP address, ads displayed and ad interactions, app interactions, and diagnostics such as crash and performance information may be sent to Google for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and consent management. Google's policies also apply to its handling of this advertising data.

Tracking permission is optional. You can still edit and save images if you decline. When tracking is not allowed, the app requests non-personalized ads. Consent forms are shown as appropriate for your region, and Ad Privacy Settings appears in the app's Settings when required. You can also change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Non-personalized ads may still involve data processing needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

Accounts and analytics

No app account is required. The developer has not added a separate analytics SDK. Advertising-related measurement by the ad SDK is described above.
