White Canvas Support

Add space around a single photo and save it as a 9:16 image.

Getting started

  1. Tap Choose Photo and select one photo.
  2. Drag to move it and pinch to resize it.
  3. Use the 90° rotation and reset rotation controls to adjust the angle. Turn off rotation lock to rotate freely with two fingers.
  4. Tap Settings (the gear icon) to choose from six background colors.
  5. Tap Save Canvas to save a 1080 × 1920 image to Photos.

If you cannot save

Check White Canvas's permission to add photos in iOS Settings. Check available device storage, select the image again, and retry.

If the angle changes while resizing

Turn on rotation lock. Reset rotation changes only the angle, keeping the position and scale. Reset restores the entire photo placement.

Advertising and privacy

Tracking permission is optional. Where required, you can change consent using Ad Privacy Settings in Settings. You can continue editing if ads fail to load.
